Method

Model. A daily crop growth model (light interception, temperature, soil water, nitrogen, heat and wetness effects, breeding progress) runs for every district from the observed sowing date. A learned correction and a data-driven gradient-boosting model refine it; satellite greenness (MODIS NDVI over cropland) enters from 2000. During the season, the weather after the forecast date is replaced by each of 39 past seasons (1979-2017), which gives the forecast distribution.

v10 additions. An annual district level update learned from the model's own past errors (district yields published two years earlier), a national 3-year update for grain maize, and a barley model refitted every 3 years to follow fast breeding progress. Ranges combine weather uncertainty with a shared year-wide error and a local error; for barley and grain maize the error size adapts to the misses of recent years.

Data. DWD HYRAS weather, DWD stations, CM SAF SARAH-3 sunlight, BGR soils, DWD phenology, Destatis district yields (regional database 41241) and Duden et al. 2024, fertiliser statistics, MODIS MOD13Q1, ESA WorldCover. Benchmarks: JRC MARS Bulletins 2009-2026, Destatis Fachserie 3 Reihe 3.2.1 2005-2022.

Validation. Every design choice was made on the training years 1979-2017 by forward validation (fit up to a year, predict the years after), mostly in two separate periods, with the rules written down before results were seen. The test years were looked at once, after freezing the model with file fingerprints. The frozen predictions for 2026 will be checked against the official 2026 district yields in spring 2027.

Known limitations. The model is still somewhat too optimistic in very dry and hot summers, above all for maize. Barley before harvest is behind MARS. At state level the Destatis harvest reporters, who observe the crop, are better. Grain maize district data end in 2021. MODIS satellites are retiring; crop-specific Sentinel-2 greenness is being added (next version). This platform shows districts; field and 10 m maps are phase 2.
