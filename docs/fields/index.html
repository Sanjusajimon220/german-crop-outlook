How the field values are made

Yield level from the district model. Each district's forecast is the released district model (v12) at the typical harvest date. The area-weighted mean of all wheat fields in a district equals that forecast exactly, so the field map never contradicts the district outlook.

Two yield layers. "Forecast" is what the outlook says at harvest time. Once Destatis publishes the official district yield (about 1.5 years later), "official district level" keeps the same field pattern but sets the level to the official figure; in the Rur the district forecast was off by about 1 t/ha in 2022, 2024 and 2025, and every field inherits that.

Pattern between fields from Sentinel-2. For every field, green leaf area (LAI) is retrieved from each clear Sentinel-2 image with a PROSAIL-trained network. The signal used is green leaf area duration in the 45 days after heading: how long the canopy stays green while the grain fills. Field factor = 1 + 0.41 × (field duration / district mean − 1), limited to ±30 %. The 0.41 comes from measured wheat fields (YieldSAT, research data). Tested on farms left out of the fit, the pattern ranks fields correctly (median r 0.50 within a district and season) but does not reduce the error in t/ha (1.000 vs 0.996 t/ha for a flat map), because the strength of the link differs between farms. Read the field-to-field differences as a ranking; the t/ha level is the district's.

Field history. The previous crop comes from the NRW subsidy records (the field of the year before that covers most of it). Wheat after potato stays green about 8 % longer than its district's median, after sugar beet 3.5 % longer, after wheat 9 % shorter and after barley 14 % shorter (2022-2025, about 66,000 field-seasons): the satellite confirms the rotation effect farmers know.

Water from the field model. A daily soil-water and crop model per field (1 km DWD weather, BK50 soil map, development from the crop calendar, weighted by the field's satellite LAI) gives the season's water demand and actual use. Supply = use / demand; below 0.85 the crop was short of water. Checked against measured evaporation (eddy covariance) on five TR32 wheat field-seasons near Jülich: the daily pattern agrees (r 0.76), but the model's water use is about 23 % lower than measured. Treat the mm values as under-estimates and use the supply ratio and the timing of stress.

Limits. Pilot: winter wheat only, Rur region only. Field yields and water values are not yet checked against measured field data in this region. The 80 % range combines the district range with the field-to-field spread that satellite data cannot explain (12 %).
